# Irregular Income Budget: demo

**[Try the demo](https://kordcreativeco.github.io/irregular-income-budget-demo/)**

A calm budget planner for anyone with a salary *and* side income, freelance work or a mix of clients. This demo runs on made-up figures, so you can click through every screen before you buy.

- **Safe to spend:** one number for the week, with your lumpy income smoothed into steady monthly pay
- **Tax vault:** your own set-aside rate, worked out from your salary and side income together
- **Six-month outlook:** see the quiet months coming, with invoices placed when each client usually pays
- **Six countries, two languages:** Australia, United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany and Austria, in English or German

The full planner keeps your own figures privately on your device, works offline once opened, and comes with a 12-page guide.
